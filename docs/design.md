# Design Direction: Corporate Hydro-Engineering Prestige

## Overview
A high-tier, authoritative digital presence for PT Kramat Hidro Mandiri. The aesthetic balances sovereign gravitas, engineering mastery, and unwavering institutional trust.

## Core Axes
- **Hero Archetype:** Immersive Water/Hydro-Atmospheric WebGL Hero (minimal 60fps)
- **Navigation:** Glassmorphic Sticky Header
- **Rhythm:** Enterprise-grade 12-column grid system
- **Card Character:** Static, glassmorphic cards with hairline borders
- **Button Character:** Structured, industrial edges (4px radius)
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (Titles) + Plus Jakarta Sans (Body)
- **Imagery Treatment:** Minimalistic, technical, data-driven
- **Motion Level:** Functional (reveals, transitions)
- **Density:** High-density, executive-tier information

## Alternatives
1. **Modernist/Minimal:** Focus on typography-heavy display (avoided).
2. **Technical-Utility:** Focus on SCADA-dashboard-style density throughout (avoided).

## Choice Rationale
The chosen direction leverages institutional dignity (Playfair) with engineering precision (Plus Jakarta) to ground the brand as the premier partner for national scale EPC projects.
