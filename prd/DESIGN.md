## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-tier, authoritative digital presence for a state-scale hydropower and electrical engineering conglomerate. The aesthetic fuses monumental infrastructure prestige—evoking institutions like Siemens Energy, GE Vernova, and ABB—with precise architectural finesse tailored for high-ranking executive stakeholders, ministry directors (Kementerian ESDM, PUPR), and state-owned enterprise (BUMN) leadership.

The emotional tone balances sovereign gravitas, engineering mastery, and unwavering institutional trust. The visual direction merges **Corporate Modernism** with sophisticated **Hydro-Atmospheric Glassmorphism**: deep abyssal oceanic navies ground the architecture, while precise hairline glass borders, translucent surfaces, and radiant champagne-gold metallic accents articulate high-value energy outputs, telemetry thresholds, and executive-tier calls to action.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for a commanding dark-mode foundation that communicates structural solidity, telemetry clarity, and national utility scale.

- **Primary (`#C9A84C` - Champagne Gold):** Reserved for primary executive actions, turbine/megawatt performance highlights, prestige seals, and decisive micro-accents. On hover/active states, transitions through `#DBBC6A` and `#B39238`.
- **Neutral Surface Baseline (`#0A1628` - Deep Abyssal Navy):** The structural canvas. Eliminates true black in favor of deep oceanic depth, maintaining ocular comfort in operations centers and boardroom displays.
- **Secondary Surface Layer (`#1A2A4A` - Slate Naval Glass):** Serves as the substrate for containers, data cards, and structural grouping.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#38BDF8` - Hydroelectric Luminescence):** An electric cyan-blue reserved strictly for live telemetry (water flow volume, turbine frequency, grid synchronization status).
- **Surface Borders & Dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`):** Sub-pixel and hairline boundaries that define technical precision without clutter.
- **Text Tiers:** Pristine pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary headings, slate ice (`#94A3B8`) for secondary descriptive copy and technical labels, and muted obsidian-slate (`#64748B`) for tertiary utility metadata.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses executive statecraft alongside German-grade engineering rigor.

- **Headlines & Executive Display:** Set in **Playfair Display**. Conveys national gravitas, institutional legacy, and sovereign dignity. Used across project portfolio names, capacity milestone highlights, and formal address sections.
- **Body, Numerical Metrics, & Technical Specs:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Ensures immaculate legibility across high-density SCADA dashboards, environmental impact assessments, and technical spec sheets.
- **Metric Numerals:** Scaled dynamically in **Plus Jakarta Sans Bold** with tight tracking (`-0.03em`) to anchor power generation metrics (e.g., `450 MW`, `99.8% Uptime`).
- **Technical Overlines & Micro-Labels:** `label-caps` is deployed in all-caps with generous kerning (`0.12em`) to categorize telemetry, governmental tender identifiers, and ISO certifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an enterprise-grade 12-column fluid grid system engineered for high-density engineering data, structural balance, and executive briefings:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max canvas width of 1440px centered, `margin-desktop` (80px), `gutter-desktop` (24px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `margin-tablet` (40px), `gutter-desktop` (24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `margin-mobile` (20px), `gutter-mobile` (16px).

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical rhythm relies strictly on 8px baseline intervals:
- **Component Interiors:** Utilize `space-sm` (12px) to `space-lg` (24px) padding.
- **Card Containers & Modules:** Padded with `space-xl` (32px) desktop / `space-lg` (24px) mobile.
- **Section Intervals:** Major structural transitions leverage `space-3xl` (64px) to `space-4xl` (96px) to communicate institutional scale and clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a sophisticated layered glass-and-naval hierarchy rather than stark drop shadows:

1. **Base Layer (L0 - Background Canvas):** Solid `#0A1628` accented with localized subtle radial water-refraction ambient glows (`radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 0%, rgba(26, 42, 74, 0.45) 0%, transparent 70%)`).
2. **Structural Glass (L1 - Static Cards & Panels):**
   - Background: `rgba(26, 42, 74, 0.55)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.36)`
3. **Interactive / Raised Glass (L2 - Hovered Modules, Drawers):**
   - Background: `rgba(26, 42, 74, 0.75)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(24px)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.35)` (Champagne gold hairline highlight)
   - Shadow: `0 16px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), 0 0 24px rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.08)`
4. **Modal & Executive Overlay (L3 - Dialogs, Command Palettes):**
   - Background: `rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.88)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(32px)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`
   - Shadow: `0 24px 64px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 1 (Soft)** shape geometry. Roundedness is disciplined and architectural, rejecting consumer-style pill shapes in favor of measured institutional contours:

- **Base Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radius. Conveys stability, structural integrity, and industrial discipline.
- **Cards, Panels, & Data Modules:** `8px` (`0.5rem`, `rounded-lg`). Keeps large infrastructural views structured and precise.
- **Executive Modals & Hero Canvas Elements:** `12px` (`0.75rem`, `rounded-xl`).
- **Telemetry Indicators & Status Dots:** Absolute circular (`rounded-full`) for standard sensor signals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Champagne Gold Solid):** Background `#C9A84C`, foreground `#0A1628` (Navy Dark), weight 600, radius 4px, uppercase tracking 0.05em. Hover state transitions to `#DBBC6A` with subtle gold bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary (Subtle Glass Outline):** Background `rgba(26, 42, 74, 0.6)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover applies border `1px solid #C9A84C` and text `#C9A84C`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No background or border. Slate ice text (`#94A3B8`) with inline gold arrow indicator (`→`) that offsets by +4px on hover.

### Glassmorphism Cards & Telemetry Pods
- Composed with `rgba(26, 42, 74, 0.55)`, `backdrop-blur: 16px`, and `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Features an optional top 1px gold micro-accent border line (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, #C9A84C, transparent)`) for primary megawatt plant overviews.

### Form Inputs & Select Fields
- Background: `rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.6)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans 14px, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- Focus state: Border becomes `1px solid #C9A84C` with ambient halo `0 0 0 2px rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Operational:** Background `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`, text `#38BDF8` with a pulsing 6px live status dot.
- **Contract / BUMN Tier Tag:** Background `rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.25)`, text `#C9A84C`, all-caps `label-caps` font.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size 18px with `2px` radius. Background `rgba(26, 42, 74, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Checked state: `#C9A84C` fill with `#0A1628` micro-check glyph.

### Executive KPI & Metric Display
- Dedicated component combining a top `label-caps` metadata header in slate-gray, an oversized Playfair or Plus Jakarta metric numeral (`450 MW`), and a bottom delta indicator featuring subtle hydro-cyan (`#38BDF8`) or emerald surge graphs.
